[1:00 — ARCHITECTURE]
(show assets/architecture_diagram.png)
Let's walk through how this works under the hood.
First, when you enter a request, it goes through the Security Checkpoint function node, which filters out prompt injections and redacts sensitive PII like emails or phone numbers.
Next, once passed, the Orchestrator node executes our main RecipeOrchestrator agent.
This orchestrator coordinates two specialized sub-agents: the RecipePlanner, which designs custom recipes, and the ShoppingListBuilder, which organizes the ingredients.
During recipe design, the RecipePlanner connects to our custom MCP server to query pantry items and check for allergy restrictions.
Finally, the proposed plan goes to the Human Review node, where the workflow halts to ask you for approval, allowing you to either finalize the plan or send feedback for revisions.
